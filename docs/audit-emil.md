# Emil-lens polish audit — DigiLocker concept

Scope: every screen at 1440×900 and 375×812, light and dark. Method: screenshots, plus timing samples of the drawer/swap/modal transitions taken with `requestAnimationFrame` in the live page, plus a tally of tokens from the source.

Hard numbers from the source:
- **20 distinct font sizes**, including half-pixel steps (12.5 / 13 / 13.5 / 14 / 14.5).
- **16 border-radius values.**
- **20 distinct durations.**
- Weights 550 / 650 / 750 are used, but the Google Fonts request only loads 400/500/600/700/800 as static weights. The browser snaps them (650 → 700, 750 → 800), so the hierarchy you see isn't the one written in the code.

Measured motion:
- Drawer enter: 496px → 0 in about 430ms with the drawer curve. Good, front-loaded.
- Drawer exit: 168px in the first 22ms. It reads as a snap or teleport, not a slide.
- Sheet drill-in swap: fades fully to opacity 0.01 at about 143ms, then fades back in. There is a blank beat, and no direction is conveyed.

## Breakthrough ideas (vanilla, single file)

1. **Living card: the shared element that travels.** Every document is one physical object. Tap → the card lifts (`scale 1.02`, shadow deepens 120ms) → flies into the sheet hero via FLIP (420ms drawer curve) → the details slide up under it (staggered 30ms). Close reverses the path back into its exact grid slot. On mobile the sheet's background app scales to `.94` with `border-radius:16px` (Vaul-style depth), so the card feels lifted *out* of the wallet.

2. **Holographic verified seal.** Replace the "Verified" pill with a 22px foil seal on each card: a `conic-gradient` iridescent foil masked to a rosette SVG. On fine pointers, a spring-smoothed `--mx/--my` (lerp 0.15 per rAF, set on the hovered card's own `style`, not a parent) drives a radial highlight and a 3–4° `rotateX/rotateY` tilt (`perspective:800px`). On touch, the seal shimmers once when the card enters the viewport. This makes "authentic original" something you *see*, not a sentence of legal text. Disabled under reduced motion.

3. **Recipient lens in the share flow.** Next to (desktop) or above (mobile) the toggles, a live mini-card shows *exactly* what the verifier will see. Fields switched off become redaction bars that sweep in with `clip-path:inset(0 100% 0 0)→inset(0)` (180ms). "Age over 18" shows a big ✓ 18+ badge where the birth date would be. On create, the lens card flips (`rotateY 180°`, 500ms) into the QR. A draining **conic ring** around the QR (`background:conic-gradient(var(--brand) calc(var(--p)*1turn),transparent 0)`, with `--p` updated every second) makes the expiry tangible.

4. **Wallet deck mode (mobile-first).** On phones, cards stack Apple-Wallet-style: each card shows only its top 64px strip (issuer + title), overlapping `margin-top:-150px`. Tap one: it rises to the top and the others compress to 12px edges at the bottom (spring 0.45s, bounce 0.15). Swipe down to return to the deck. This turns 17 documents into one screen instead of seven screens of scrolling. On desktop it can be a "Deck / Grid" toggle.

5. **The arrival moment.** When a fetch succeeds, don't just swap to a success state. The new card slides down from the sheet into its category row (FLIP from the success hero to the grid slot as the sheet closes), and a seal "stamps" onto it (`scale 1.3 → 1`, `rotate -12° → 0`, 260ms ease-out, with a 1px ink-spread ring). On Android add `navigator.vibrate?.(12)`. The suggestions strip then shrinks by one (grid-row collapse). This happens once per document, so it's allowed to be delightful.
